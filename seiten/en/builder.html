Design Builder

Colours, corners, fonts and the position of every module — live over a preview in HUD pixels. Whatever you like goes into the overlay as design.json and a layout.

Into the overlay

On the HUD in three steps.

The builder writes exactly the files KERS reads. The preview is a recreation — sizes, colours and positions match, the real HUD shows the finer details.

01

Create the design

Start KERS, /settings → Design: pick the base you used in the builder (KERS or F1 TV), enter a name and Copy. Now data\designs\<name> sits next to the EXE.

02

Replace design.json

Put the downloaded design.json into that folder, overwriting the old one. Press Reload on the control panel — done. Your own fonts go into fonts\ next to it.

03

Apply the layout

Copy the command under Export, open the console in /settings with F12, paste, Enter. The layout applies at once and is listed under Custom layouts. No console: put layouts.json into data\ and restart KERS.

Everything stays with you. The builder uploads nothing — not even a background image. Your work is only remembered in this browser; to pass it on, use the link under Export.
