The modules

Each one calculates for itself, can be switched off individually and placed freely. Whatever has nothing to show stays quiet.

Thirteen parts. You decide which ones run.

Timing Tower

The running order with gap to the leader, interval, sectors, tyres and DRS. Position changes glide, penalties hang off the row as a pill.

Trackmap

The circuit is recognised by its ID and the outline preloaded — only what is missing gets learned, and what you drove yourself beats what shipped. Rotation and mirroring are remembered per circuit. Every car as a dot, the camera driver highlighted.

Meldungs-Banner

Penalties, flags, safety car and investigations queue up across one shared bar.

Fastest-Lap-Banner

The purple banner when the fastest lap falls — with driver, team and time.

Start-Ampel

The five start lights, in sync with the game's start procedure.

Battle-Boxen

Detects groups fighting and projects when the driver behind will actually be there — including undercut detection during pit stops.

Hotlap-Boxen

In qualifying: who is on a flying lap right now, sector by sector against the best time.

Lower-Third

The name tag on a camera change — driver, team, position.

Gefahrenzone

Countdown for the last safe spot: who drops out if the session ended now.

Onboard

Telemetry of the camera driver: gear, revs, throttle, brake, ERS, tyre condition.

Pit-Projektion

“Where would he come out?” — the position the driver would fall back to if he pitted now.

Boxenstopp-Karten

A live stationary timer as soon as a car is in the pit box.

WM-Stand

Drivers' and teams' standings from your own championship file, shown on demand from the control page.
