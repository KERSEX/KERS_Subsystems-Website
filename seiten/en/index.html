Your race,
in numbers.

KERS Subsystems listens to the telemetry stream from F1 26 and turns it into an overlay that makes the situation readable at a glance: timing tower, track map, battles, pit stops, penalties. As a frameless window on top of the game — and as its own window for recording in OBS.

On top of the game

A frameless window, always on top. Locked, every click passes through — you keep playing normally while the overlay sits above it.

In OBS

A second, opaque window at canvas size. Pick it up in OBS with a window capture, colour key on magenta — done.

From your phone

Control and settings are the two pages in the browser. On the same network you steer the overlay from your phone — right down to the camera in the game.
