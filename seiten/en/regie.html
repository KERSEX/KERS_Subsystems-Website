The control page

A second page that never goes on screen — from here you steer what is to be seen.

Under /regie runs a second page from which nothing goes into the picture: the whole field with tyres, gaps and pit stops, race control, a pace comparison and the live projection of the championship. From here you show and hide what is due — and, if you like, control OBS at the same time.

01

Director’s Eye

The camera hint tells you where something is happening — which duel, which lap, which stop is worth the look.

02

Tap the driver

Every row of the leaderboard is the button itself: tap it and the camera in the game jumps to that driver — the row flashes briefly. Who you are on is shown by the 🎥 in the row. From your phone just the same.

03

Cut-ins

Charts, championship standings, battle and hotlap boxes can be brought on screen by hand instead of waiting for the automatic.

04

Twitch chat alongside

Enter a channel name or link and the chat slides open on the right — with the stream muted above it if you like. By default KERS reads the chat directly, which also works in browsers that block the Twitch embed. One button switches back to the embed.

05

Control OBS

Switch on the WebSocket server in OBS and connect in /settings under OBS control — the control page then shows the OBS card: scenes, sources, audio, transitions, stream and recording. Docks picks which parts appear there; your own browser docks from OBS come along too.

Going live always asks first. The OBS password stays encrypted on the PC running KERS.

06

Switch the standings

The WM: Fahrer button switches the standings on screen between drivers, teams and both in turn — with the points system you set in the settings.

Access and camera: the camera control sends key presses into the running game. That is why only this PC gets into the control page as it is — every other device enters the 6-digit code from the control panel once on its first visit (Access). Under Devices … any device can be blocked again; an open control page is thrown out right away. The camera keys can also be switched off entirely in /settings. Switching the code off opens the control page to every device on the network again.
