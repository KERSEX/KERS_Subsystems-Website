The control page

A second page that never goes on screen — from here you steer what is to be seen.

Under /regie runs a second page from which nothing goes into the picture: the whole field with tyres, gaps and pit stops, race control, a pace comparison and the live projection of the championship. From here you show and hide what is due.

01

Director’s Eye

The camera hint tells you where something is happening — which duel, which lap, which stop is worth the look.

02

Tap the driver

Every row of the leaderboard is the button itself: tap it and the camera in the game jumps to that driver — the row flashes briefly. Who you are on is shown by the 🎥 in the row. From your phone just the same.

03

Cut-ins

Charts, championship standings, battle and hotlap boxes can be brought on screen by hand instead of waiting for the automatic.

About the camera control: it sends key presses into the running game, and the server listens on the whole network. As long as the switch is on, anyone on the same Wi-Fi can move the camera. The endpoint only accepts a position number — which key that is, the server looks up itself — but the real protection is the switch: it can be turned off in /settings and is on by default.
