What sets it apart

Showing gaps is something every overlay can do. It gets interesting when that turns into a prediction.

Not just what is.
Also what comes.

  • Fight in three laps. The overlay detects groups fighting and works out from lap times and gap when the driver behind will really be there. The countdown is in the battle box before the duel is on screen.
  • Undercut detected. If a pit stop flips the order, the message banner says so — not afterwards, but while the stop is running.
  • Where would he come out? The pit projection puts the driver into the pits in theory and shows the position he would drop back to.
  • The last safe spot. In qualifying the danger zone runs a countdown for whoever would be knocked out right now.
