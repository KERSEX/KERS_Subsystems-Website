Free layout

A module doesn't hang on fixed pixels but on one of nine anchor points plus an offset.

What you place at the bottom right stays at the bottom right — even when the HUD runs at 2560×1440 and OBS records at 1920×1080.

  • With the mouse. “Edit layout” on the control panel: every module gets a frame with its name, you drag it into place and resize it from the corners. Whatever shows nothing right now sits there as a labelled placeholder.
  • Pixel exact. In the settings every module has sliders for anchor point, offset, size and layer.
  • What sits in front. The Layers list stacks the modules — top is front. It really only changes the layer: anything still on default keeps its built-in place.
  • Several arrangements. Under My layouts you save the current arrangement under a name — one for the race, one for qualifying. They live on the server, so they are there from your phone too.
  • Back to the start. “Default layout” restores every position — branding, colours and the rest stay untouched.
