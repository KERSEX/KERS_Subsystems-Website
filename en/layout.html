Layout & designs

A module doesn't hang on fixed pixels but on one of nine anchor points plus an offset.

What you place at the bottom right stays at the bottom right — even when the HUD runs at 2560×1440 and OBS records at 1920×1080.

  • With the mouse. “Edit layout” on the control panel: every module gets a frame with its name, you drag it into place and resize it from the corners. Whatever shows nothing right now sits there as a labelled placeholder.
  • Pixel exact. In the settings every module has sliders for anchor point, offset, size and layer.
  • What sits in front. The Layers list stacks the modules — top is front. It really only changes the layer: anything still on default keeps its built-in place.
  • Several arrangements. Under My layouts you save the current arrangement under a name — one for the race, one for qualifying. They live on the server, so they are there from your phone too.
  • Back to the start. “Default layout” restores every position — branding, colours and the rest stay untouched.

Designs

Two designs. And yours.

What the HUD and the OBS window look like is set by a design — chosen in /settings under Design.

  • KERS. The look it all started with: tower with sectors and DRS, battle boxes, track map.
  • F1 TV. A recreation of the F1 TV graphics: tower with full names and a red P1, “LAP 32 / 57”, yellow safety car banner, tyres as a round disc with their age in laps. Gap and interval take turns every 8 s, a purple stopwatch marks the fastest lap. Instead of an F1 logo, your own from the branding settings sits on top.
  • Target time like on TV. In qualifying, whoever would go through runs against pole — whoever would drop out runs against the time at the cut line, as a split at the next sector. After the line the card shows time, delta and new position for 5 s.
  • The camera driver gets a brighter row with a white stripe, a small arrow next to the tower and a white frame around their hot lap card.
  • Everything in the same style: battle boxes, fastest lap, “DROP ZONE”, “RACE CONTROL” and “STRATEGY”, the standings with live points and “PIT STOP” with stationary time, tyre change and exit position.
  • Your own design. In /settings, copy a design as your own. The folder data\designs\<name> then holds design.json (colours, opacity, corners, fonts), a folder for your own fonts and the complete scene; every file there replaces the built-in one. Then press Reload on the control panel.
